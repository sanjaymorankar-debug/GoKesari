2. Appointment and right to list and sell

2.1 The Platform grants the Seller a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to list, display, offer and sell its products on gokesari.com and the GoKesari mobile apps, during the term of this agreement.

2.2 The Seller is the seller of record. Title in the goods passes from the Seller to the Customer on delivery. The Platform acts only as a marketplace intermediary and does not own or hold the Seller's inventory.

2.3 The Seller sets its own prices, discounts and stock levels. Prices must not exceed the MRP printed on the product.

2.4 The Seller shall list only products it is legally allowed to sell, that are genuine, and that match their photos and descriptions. Banned, counterfeit, expired, recalled or misbranded goods must never be listed.

2.5 The Seller shall issue a GST-compliant tax invoice (or bill of supply) in its own name for every order and send it with the goods or digitally to the Customer.

Additional rules for medicines

  • Prescription-only medicines (Schedule H, H1 and X) are dispensed only against a valid prescription, checked by the Seller's registered pharmacist before packing.
  • Schedule X and other narcotic or psychotropic drugs are not sold through gokesari.com.
  • Medicines are supplied in original sealed packs, with batch number and expiry date visible, and at least 3 months of shelf life remaining unless the Customer agrees otherwise.
  • Cold-chain products (insulin, vaccines, some eye drops) are packed with ice gel packs or insulated pouches and handed over within the cold-chain time stated on the pack.

3.1 Business hours. The Seller keeps its listed opening hours accurate and switches the shop to Offline when closed. Orders received while the shop shows Online are the Seller's responsibility.

3.2 Stock accuracy. The Seller updates stock in real time, or at least twice a day for slow-moving items. An order rejected for no stock counts as a seller-fault cancellation.

3.3 Partial availability. If one item is out of stock, the Seller may offer a substitute of equal or higher quality at the same or lower price, through the app, before packing. The Customer must approve any substitute. Medicines are never substituted without the Customer's and the prescription's approval.

3.4 Choosing the fulfilment mode. When marking the order Ready, the Seller selects one of:

  1. Customer pickup at the time the Customer chose;
  2. the Seller's own delivery staff, naming the delivery person and expected delivery time; or
  3. a gokesari.com delivery partner, who is assigned automatically.

3.5 Handover. For a gokesari.com delivery partner, the packed order must be ready at the counter when the partner arrives. If the partner waits more than 5 minutes because the order is not ready, it counts as a late dispatch.

4.1 Seller's own delivery. When the Seller delivers, it is fully responsible for on-time delivery and for the goods' condition until the OTP is entered. Delivery staff must carry the gokesari.com order details and must not ask the Customer for any amount beyond the order total.

4.2 Platform delivery partner. When a gokesari.com delivery partner delivers, the Seller is responsible up to handover. Delays or damage after handover are the Platform's responsibility, unless caused by the Seller's packing.

4.3 Customer pickup. The order must be ready at the counter at the chosen time and held for at least 2 hours. An order not collected within 24 hours may be cancelled under the refund policy.

4.4 Delays. If the Seller expects a delay, it must update the order status in the app before the window ends so the Customer is told in real time. A delay notified in advance still counts as late, but is weighted at half in the late-delivery rate.

4.5 Failed delivery. If the Customer cannot be reached after 2 call attempts and a 10-minute wait, the order is returned to the Seller and handled under the cancellation policy. This is not a seller-fault event.

4.6 Force majeure. Delays caused by heavy rain, floods, curfews, strikes, network outages or other events outside the Seller's control are excluded from the metrics, if reported in the app at the time.

5. Packaging and condition of goods

Every order must reach the Customer complete, correct, clean, sealed and fit for use. The Seller is responsible for any defect that arises from the goods themselves or from the Seller's packing.

General rules (all orders)

  • Pack in a clean, closed bag or box sealed with tape or a tamper-evident sticker showing the gokesari.com order number.
  • Check every item against the order before sealing: product, brand, size, quantity, and MRP.
  • Place the invoice inside the pack or send it digitally.
  • Do not use newspaper or printed paper in direct contact with food.
  • Do not mix food with detergents, chemicals or strong-smelling products in the same inner bag.

Escalation ladder when a section 6 target is missed

  1. First miss: dashboard warning, plus email and WhatsApp to the Seller with the orders involved.
  2. Second miss within 30 days: lower ranking in search and "Nearby" results for 7 days.
  3. Third miss within 60 days: orders paused for 3 days.
  4. Fourth miss within 90 days: orders paused for 7 days, and a call with the support team to agree a fix plan.
  5. Continued misses, or any fraud, counterfeit, or serious medicine breach: termination of this agreement and removal from gokesari.com.

8.1 Recovery. A Seller that meets every target for 30 days in a row returns to step 0 and full search ranking.

8.2 Rewards. Sellers that meet every target for 90 days get a "GoKesari Trusted Shop" badge and priority in search.

8.3 Appeal. The Seller may appeal any charge or pause within 7 days through the seller support page. The Platform decides appeals within 5 business days.

Customer complaints are acknowledged within 48 hours and resolved within one month, as required by the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.

11.3 Indemnity. The Seller indemnifies the Platform against claims, fines and losses arising from its products, licences, taxes, or breach of this agreement or the law.

11.4 Liability. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss. The Platform's total liability is limited to the commission it earned from the Seller in the previous 3 months.

11.5 Data. The Seller uses customer names, phone numbers and addresses only to fulfil gokesari.com orders. It must not store, sell or use them for any other purpose.

11.6 Changes. The Platform may update this SLA with 30 days' notice by email and on the seller dashboard. Continuing to sell after that date means the Seller accepts the change.
