Recitals

The Company owns and operates gokesari.com, an online marketplace (website and mobile applications for Android and iOS) that connects registered sellers with buyers for the sale and purchase of goods and services, and acts as an intermediary within the meaning of Section 2(1)(w) of the Information Technology Act, 2000 and a marketplace e-commerce entity under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.

B. The Merchant runs a licensed retail (and, where stated, wholesale) pharmacy and holds valid licences under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and the Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945, and employs a pharmacist registered under the Pharmacy Act, 1948.

C. The Merchant has applied to be onboarded on gokesari.com to list, sell and purchase medicines and allied products, and the Company has agreed to authorise the Merchant on the terms below.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions

  1. “Platform” means gokesari.com, its sub-domains, its Android and iOS mobile applications and any associated seller, operator and admin dashboards.
  2. “Products” means medicines (allopathic, Ayurvedic, Siddha, Unani and homoeopathic), OTC products, medical devices, surgical items, nutraceuticals, cosmetics and other healthcare goods that the Merchant is lawfully licensed to sell.
  3. “Prescription Drugs” means drugs listed in Schedule H and Schedule H1 of the Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945, and any other drug that by law may be sold only on a prescription of a Registered Medical Practitioner.
  4. “Prohibited Products” means drugs in Schedule X, narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances under the NDPS Act, 1985, banned fixed-dose combinations, spurious, adulterated, misbranded or expired drugs, and any product that the Merchant is not licensed to sell.
  5. “Customer” means any person who places an order on the Platform.
  6. “Merchant Wallet” means the prepaid wallet maintained by the Merchant on the Platform from which fees, commission and delivery charges are deducted.
  7. “Order” means a confirmed purchase request placed by a Customer and accepted by the Merchant through the Platform.

2. Authorisation and Scope

  1. Subject to this Agreement, the Company authorises the Merchant, on a non-exclusive, non-transferable and revocable basis, to register on the Platform and to list, display, sell and purchase Products through the Platform.
  2. The Merchant may (a) sell Products to Customers (B2C), and (b) where it holds a wholesale licence, sell to and purchase from other licensed pharmacies, distributors and manufacturers registered on the Platform (B2B).
  3. The Company acts only as a technology platform and intermediary. The contract of sale of any Product is directly between the Merchant and the buyer. The Company does not take title to, stock, dispense or prescribe any Product.
  4. Onboarding is complete when (a) the registration fee is paid, (b) the documents in Annexure A are uploaded and verified, and (c) the Company issues a confirmation by email or in-app. The Company may refuse or put on hold any application without assigning reasons.

3. Licences and Documents

  1. The Merchant shall upload, and keep valid at all times, the documents listed in Annexure A.
  2. The Merchant shall update the Platform within 7 days of any renewal, suspension, cancellation or change of a licence, pharmacist, ownership or address.
  3. If any licence expires or is suspended, the Merchant shall immediately stop selling the affected Products. The Company may delist the Merchant or the affected Products without notice.

4. Regulatory Compliance

The Merchant shall comply with all applicable law, including the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and Rules, 1945; the Pharmacy Act, 1948; the NDPS Act, 1985; the Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act, 1954; the Drugs (Prices Control) Order, 2013; the Legal Metrology Act, 2009; GST laws; the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and E-Commerce Rules, 2020; and any e-pharmacy rules notified by the Government of India. In particular:

  1. Prescriptions. Prescription Drugs shall be dispensed only against a valid prescription from a Registered Medical Practitioner uploaded by the Customer. The Merchant’s registered pharmacist shall verify each prescription before dispatch and may reject any doubtful, incomplete or expired prescription.
  2. Schedule H1 records. For Schedule H1 drugs, the Merchant shall keep a separate register with the prescriber, patient, drug name and quantity, and retain it for at least 3 years.
  3. Prohibited Products. The Merchant shall not list or sell any Prohibited Product. The Platform will block such listings, and any attempt is a material breach.
  4. Pricing. No Product shall be sold above its MRP or the ceiling price notified by NPPA. Discounts are at the Merchant’s discretion, within law.
  5. Quality and storage. The Merchant shall sell only genuine Products sourced from licensed suppliers, with at least {{fees.medicineShelfLifeMonths}} months of shelf life at delivery, and shall maintain cold-chain storage and packaging where required.
  6. Invoices. Every Order shall carry a GST invoice showing batch number, expiry date, manufacturer and the pharmacist’s name.
  7. Advertising. The Merchant shall not make any claim about cure or treatment in listings, and shall not offer medical advice to Customers through the Platform.
  8. Recalls. On any recall or not-of-standard-quality alert from CDSCO or a State Drug Controller, the Merchant shall delist the batch at once and inform affected Customers through the Platform.

6. Orders, Fulfilment, Delivery and Returns

  1. The Merchant shall accept or reject each Order within {{fees.acceptMinutes}} minutes, based on stock and prescription verification. A rejected Order is refunded to the Customer in full.
  2. For each accepted Order the Merchant chooses one fulfilment mode: (a) customer pickup at a scheduled time, (b) delivery by the Merchant’s own staff, or (c) delivery by a gokesari.com delivery partner.
  3. Prescription Drugs shall be packed in sealed, tamper-evident packaging with the invoice. The delivery person shall not open or substitute the pack.
  4. An Order is complete when the delivery OTP is entered by the delivery person, or the Customer confirms pickup.
  5. Cancellations. An Order cancelled before packing is refunded in full, including the delivery fee.
  6. Returns. Returns are accepted only for damaged, wrong, expired or near-expiry items reported within 48 hours of delivery, with photos. Opened medicines, cold-chain items and Prescription Drugs that were correctly supplied are not returnable, except where required by law.
  7. Disputes. Customer complaints raise a ticket to the Merchant and the support team. The Merchant shall respond within 24 hours and resolve within 7 days.

7. Listings and Catalogue

  1. The Merchant may use the Platform’s master product data (with MRP) or add its own listings with photos and descriptions. The Merchant is responsible for the accuracy of name, composition, strength, pack size, MRP, manufacturer and Schedule classification.
  2. Any MRP or price changed by the Merchant applies only to that Merchant’s shop.
  3. The Merchant shall keep stock levels updated so that out-of-stock items are not ordered.
  4. The Merchant grants the Company a royalty-free licence to display its name, logo, shop photos and listings on the Platform and in its marketing for the term of this Agreement.

8. Company’s Obligations

  1. Provide the Merchant with a seller dashboard and mobile app access to manage listings, Orders, wallet, settlements and reports.
  2. Provide prescription upload and verification workflow, payment gateway, and delivery partner services where opted.
  3. Provide support through the Platform support page, email and phone during business hours.
  4. Block Prohibited Products and act on any complaint or order of a government authority as required of an intermediary.

9. Data Protection and Confidentiality

  1. Customer data, including prescriptions and health information, is personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. The Merchant shall use it only to fulfil the relevant Order, shall not store it outside the Platform except as required by law (such as the Schedule H1 register), and shall not use it for marketing.
  2. Each Party shall keep confidential the other’s business information, pricing and data, during the term and for 2 years after it ends.
  3. The Merchant shall report any data breach or misuse to the Company within 24 hours.

10. Representations and Indemnity

  1. The Merchant represents that all licences and documents furnished are genuine and valid, and that all Products are genuine and lawfully sourced.
  2. The Merchant shall indemnify and keep indemnified the Company, its directors, employees and delivery partners against all claims, losses, penalties, costs and legal fees arising from (a) any Product sold, including adverse effects, spurious, expired or wrong dispensing; (b) dispensing without a valid prescription; (c) breach of law or of this Agreement; or (d) incorrect listing information.
  3. The Company shall not be liable for the quality, safety, efficacy or dispensing of Products. The Company’s total liability to the Merchant under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid by the Merchant in the 3 months before the claim.

11. Term, Suspension and Termination

  1. This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for 1 (one) year, renewing automatically for further one-year terms unless either Party gives 30 days’ notice.
  2. Either Party may terminate without cause on 30 days’ written notice.
  3. The Company may suspend or terminate immediately if the Merchant (a) sells Prohibited Products or dispenses without prescription, (b) has a licence expired, suspended or cancelled, (c) commits fraud or misrepresentation, (d) receives repeated Customer complaints, or (e) breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within 7 days of notice.
  4. On termination, the Merchant shall complete or cancel and refund pending Orders. The Company shall settle the wallet balance and dues within 30 days, after deducting amounts owed. Clauses 9, 10 and 12 survive termination.

12. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

  1. This Agreement is governed by the laws of India.
  2. Disputes shall first be resolved by mutual discussion within 30 days. Failing this, they shall be referred to a sole arbitrator appointed by the Company under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The seat and venue shall be Pune, and the language English.
  3. Subject to arbitration, courts at Pune, Maharashtra shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

13. General

  1. Entire agreement. This Agreement, its Annexures and the Platform’s seller policies (as updated from time to time) form the entire agreement. If they conflict, this Agreement prevails.
  2. Assignment. The Merchant shall not assign this Agreement without the Company’s prior written consent.
  3. Notices. Notices shall be in writing to the addresses and emails above, or through the Platform dashboard.
  4. Force majeure. Neither Party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control.
  5. Independent parties. Nothing here creates a partnership, agency or employment relationship.
  6. Electronic execution. This Agreement may be accepted electronically on the Platform, which shall be valid under the Information Technology Act, 2000.
  7. Mode of execution. This Agreement is executed and accepted electronically on the Platform by the Merchant’s OTP-based e-signature and acceptance of its terms, and such acceptance constitutes the Merchant’s agreement to these terms. It is not executed on stamp paper.
