# Dealer, team lead and agent guide

03-Nov-2026. Your page: **gokesari.com/dealer** (also in the GoKesari app). Only when GoKesari has
moved your dealership to the 3-level programme.

## Who earns what

On every shop that pays its GoKesari registration fee (before GST, after any coupon):

* whoever signed the shop — **20%**: 60% of it when the shop is approved, the rest when it delivers
  10 orders within 90 days (shown as *trading progress*);
* the agent's team lead — **5%**, when the shop is trading;
* the main dealer — **3%** on every shop his team signs, when the shop is trading;
* on a membership renewal of a shop you signed — **10%**.

No joining fee, and nothing is paid for recruiting anyone.

## Before you are paid

1. Accept the **agent agreement** on your dealer page (what you earn before that waits on hold).
2. Add your **PAN** and **bank account**; GoKesari verifies the account.
3. Payouts are monthly (the 5th), one per PAN — if you earn in two roles you get one transfer.
   Missing details: the month waits and you get a reminder.

## Your page shows

Your code and share link; your team (main dealers and team leads); earnings by type and level;
each shop's trading progress; monthly statements with **gross, TDS and net**; your TDS (PAN status,
rate, TDS this year) and **Form 16A** downloads. Agents ask for a sub-agent from this page; GoKesari
decides.

## Shop owners

A shop has its own referral code and link on *My Shop → Registration and fee*. A shop that joins with
it gets 10% off its registration fee; you get ₹200 of credit (for GoKesari commission and delivery
charges) once it is approved and delivers its first order.
