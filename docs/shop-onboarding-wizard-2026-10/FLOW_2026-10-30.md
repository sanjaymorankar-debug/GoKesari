# Shop registration flow — owner brief of 30 Oct 2026

The onboarding wizard at `/shop/register` (flag `shop_onboarding_wizard_enabled`) now follows the
owner's brief of 30 Oct 2026. Decisions: [decision log D-F01 … D-F07](../decision-log.md#shop-registration-flow-30-oct-2026-owner-brief).
It builds on [BRIEF_REDESIGN.md](BRIEF_REDESIGN.md) (29 Oct); everything not listed here is unchanged.

## The flow

```
Sign in (code by email) ─► 1 Referral code ─► 2 Shop email ─► 3 Documents handy?
                                                                   │
                     ┌──────────── Yes ────────────────────────────┤──────── No, upload later ───────┐
                     ▼                                                                               ▼
   4 Verify: type the numbers  or  scan / upload copies                          5 Shop details & address
     GSTIN (or PAN + no-GST declaration) → Udyam → FSSAI                          (FSSAI for a food shop)
     checked online; GST record fills name, PAN, address                                     │
                     ▼                                                                       │
   5 Shop details & address (pre-filled) ◄───────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                     ▼
   6 Location (map pin) ─► 7 Review & pay ─► 8 Live ─► 9 Category ─► 10 Products
```

| # | Tile | What happens | Server step / route |
|---|---|---|---|
| — | Sign in | Signed in as a customer: the shop is added to that account (banner "Signed in as …"). Signed out: sign-in card on the page itself — Google, or mobile / email with a code **sent by email** (a new mobile number is asked for its email). Then `/onboarding` → back here | `ShopSignInCard`, `src/server/otp/service.ts` |
| 1 | Referral | Code from the link (`?ref=` / `?code=`) pre-filled; checked now; mandatory / optional by Admin → Referral settings; "Request one" | `REFERRAL`, `POST …/registration-draft/referral` |
| 2 | Email | "Use *account email*" (no code) or "Use a different email" → 6-digit code emailed → confirmed | `EMAIL`, `POST …/email` (`useAccount` / `email` / `email + code`) |
| 3 | Documents | List: GSTIN or PAN (needed), FSSAI (food shops), Udyam (optional), Shop Act and bank account (after go-live). **Yes, I have them** / **No, I'll upload them later** | `DOCS_READY`, `POST …/documents-ready` |
| 4 | Verify | Two tabs. **Type the numbers**: GST? → GSTIN, else PAN + declaration. **Scan or upload**: photos read on the device (`readInBrowser`, `detectUploadType`, `extractDocumentFields`); the owner checks / corrects each number; then GSTIN (or PAN), Udyam, FSSAI are checked online one after another. The GST record fills shop name, legal name, owner, PAN, address | `IDENTITY`, `UDYAM`, `FSSAI` (existing routes) |
| 5 | Shop details | Name, owner, mobile, shop type, address, turnover slab (fee), coupon. Pre-filled after a verified GSTIN; blank on "upload later". A food shop type is sent to FSSAI | `DETAILS` |
| 6 | Location | Map pin (or coordinates) — now **before** payment, saved on the shop when it is created | `LOCATION`, `POST …/location` |
| 7 | Payment | Review with "Change" links → "Create my shop and pay" → gateway | `REVIEW` → `PAYMENT` |
| 8 | Live | Approved on the gateway's confirmation. "Upload later": payouts held until the GSTIN or PAN is verified | `WAITING` / approved |
| 9, 10 | Category, Products | As before | `CATEGORY`, `PRODUCTS` |

A draft started before this change (GSTIN / PAN or details already given) skips tiles 1–3 and
resumes where it was.

## "Upload later" (D-F05, D-F06)

- Approval: paid through the gateway, no likely duplicate, FSSAI VERIFIED for a food shop — no
  GSTIN / PAN (`shop_kyc_complete`, migration 0120; `missingMandatoryDocuments`; `decideGovAutoApproval`;
  `shopDocumentGate` lets the fee be paid).
- Payouts: `decideShopSettlement` refuses approve / process / pay with `PAYOUT_HELD` until a GSTIN
  or PAN is VERIFIED on the shop (or a document waiver exists).
- Chasing: the 1st / 15th follow-up (`shop-document-follow-up.ts`) reminds the owner of PAN, GSTIN
  and Shop Act, and suspends the shop after the third reminder.
- A GSTIN / PAN tried and refused before the owner switched to "later" is not carried to the shop.
