- **Paying the fee with Cashfree (case 3):** no Cashfree session on staging (E-04); still pending the owner's separate decision. The fee was settled instead through the owner's waiver flow (SD-03w), which also showed the shop staying PENDING_APPROVAL with the fee settled. A waiver and a payment are alternatives for one shop (a waiver is refused once anything is paid), so SD-03 needs a new QA shop once E-04 is decided. Not checked: whether a notice reached the owner about the waiver, and the dealer commission on referral E2ETEST1 (release #275: a waived fee earns none).
- **Approve on promise → APPROVED (case 4), the shop under Live (case 5) and the owner's live dashboard:** the fee is now settled (waived), so only the click remains. The QA agent does not approve shops on staging (its rule 2) unless the owner tells it so directly; the go-ahead came relayed through another agent and the owner's quoted words speak of the fee waiver only. To finish: an operator (or the QA agent, on the owner's own explicit instruction) clicks Approve on promise → Confirm approval for "QA Docs 2026-10-29 mvtcd0sw". Then re-run `-g "SD-05|SD-07"` and check: status APPROVED; the waiver "Owner promised the documents by 28 Nov 2026; approved after inspection"; the "shop approved" notice to the owner; the shop under Live with GSTIN and Shop Act missing, "Reminders: 0 of 3", "promised by 28 Nov 2026" and no longer under Awaiting approval; the owner's dashboard without the approval alert, and the verification page still listing GSTIN and Shop Act.
- **Long form in the browser:** the shop was registered through the long form's own endpoint (`POST /api/shops`) rather than by filling the form. Google Maps did not load in the test browser ("This page didn't load Google Maps correctly"), and location confirmation is part of the form.
- **Wizard review step (`review-documents`, promise box, "Create my shop and pay" disabled):** optional and skipped, because it would create admin-owned data on staging.
