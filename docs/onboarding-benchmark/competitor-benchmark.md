# Seller / partner onboarding — competitor benchmark

Prepared 05-Nov-2026, 23.15 IST, for the Phase A onboarding audit. Companion docs:
[current-flow.md](current-flow.md) · [gap-analysis.md](gap-analysis.md) · [proposed-flow.md](proposed-flow.md).

## How to read this

- **Public sources only.** No account was created and no credentials were entered on any platform.
- **Not reachable from this session:** sellers.blinkit.com (did not resolve), supplier.meesho.com and
  meesho.io (403), zomato.com partner page (timed out), partner.swiggy.com (empty shell),
  help.mystore.in (404). Facts for those come from press, official blogs reached through search, or
  third-party guides. A search-engine snippet of an official page is marked **(snippet)**.
- **3rd-party** = vendor/consultant blog (Unicommerce, Shiprocket, etc.) — weak evidence.
- **unverified** = no official source or reputable news report found.
- Quick-commerce players (Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart, BigBasket) run dark stores supplied by brands and
  vendors; a neighbourhood shop **cannot self-onboard** as a storefront there. They are benchmarked for
  their seller tooling, not as a like-for-like shop flow. The closest like-for-like peers for GoKesari are
  **Amazon Local Shops, Mystore/ONDC, magicpin, Meesho, Dukaan and the Swiggy/Zomato restaurant flows**.

## 1. Per-platform notes

### Quick commerce

**Blinkit (brand / seller partner)**
- Model: supplies a dark-store network. Oct 2024: self-serve **Seller Hub** for brands — inventory per dark
  store, catalogue, pricing, ads; "completely self-serve for brands" (CTO) —
  [Inc42, Oct 2024](https://inc42.com/buzz/blinkit-launches-seller-hub-to-help-brands-list-on-the-platform),
  [YourStory, Oct 2024](https://cwv.yourstory.com/2024/10/blinkit-launches-sellers-hub-zomato-quick-commerce).
- Steps (3rd-party): partner portal → business details & categories → documents → category approval →
  Seller Hub — [Unicommerce 2026](https://unicommerce.com/blog/how-to-sell-on-blinkit-seller-onboarding-guide-2026/).
- Documents, all upfront (3rd-party): PAN, GST certificate, cancelled cheque, business registration, FSSAI
  for food, brand authorisation, APOB — [myjar 2025-26](https://www.myjar.app/?p=16742).
- Fees: no registration fee; category commission (rates unverified). Time to live 7–45 days (3rd-party,
  sources disagree). Assistance: account managers + agencies. KYC method, bank check, resume, languages:
  unverified. Fit for small shops: low ("highly selective", 3rd-party).

**Zepto**
- Brand/vendor supply for dark stores; no official self-serve launch found for 2025–26.
- Steps (3rd-party): partner portal → documents → product approval → logistics —
  [Shiprocket](https://www.shiprocket.in/blog/sell-on-zepto/). Documents upfront: GST, bank, ID & address
  proof, FSSAI, catalogue images. Commission 8–25% (unverified); no registration fee (3rd-party); 3–15
  working days (3rd-party). Fit for small shops: low.

**Swiggy Instamart**
- Curated: category team evaluates each brand on demand, catalogue gaps, price —
  [Unicommerce](https://unicommerce.com/blog/sell-on-swiggy-instamart-vendor-registration/) (3rd-party).
- Entry via partner.swiggy.com/instamart or a pitch to a category manager. Documents: GSTIN (mandatory),
  PAN, FSSAI, brand authorisation, APOB. No registration fee but brand bears damage/expiry write-offs.
  6 weeks–4 months to live (all 3rd-party).
- Dec 2025 pilot: seller-run offline experience store in Gurugram —
  [Outlook Business, Dec 2025](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/start-up/e-commerce/swiggy-instamart-opens-offline-experiential-store-in-gurugram).

**Flipkart Minutes**
- Flipkart dark stores; one report says it also partners with local grocers —
  [ThirdEyesight](https://www.thirdeyesight.in/?p=6611); brands approached directly in 2024 —
  [exchange4media, Aug 2024](https://www.exchange4media.com/digital-news/d2c-brands-queue-up-for-a-minute-on-flipkart-136241.html).
- A "Sell on Flipkart Minutes" app covers registration, KYC, listing and orders on the phone
  ([Shiprocket](https://www.shiprocket.in/blog/sell-on-flipkart-minutes), 3rd-party). 10–15 min to upload
  documents, same day–1 day to verify (3rd-party); another source says 2–4 weeks
  ([SW Cybernetics](https://swcybernetics.in/knowledge-base/how-to-sell-on-flipkart-minutes-complete-guide)).
  Commission 5–20% + ₹2–5/item handling (unverified).

**BigBasket (vendor)**
- Portal partner.bigbasket.com ("BB Sambandh"); official [partner login](https://www.bigbasket.com/partner/login/).
- Steps (3rd-party): interest form → document check → category approval → listing → price review →
  **SKU / barcode mapping** → distributor-to-FC mapping — [Shiprocket](https://www.shiprocket.in/blog/sell-bigbasket-guide/).
  Documents: GST, PAN, cancelled cheque, FSSAI, trademark. ~10–15 days verify + ~1 week catalogue.

### E-commerce

**Amazon Seller Central India (incl. Local Shops, Smart Stores)**
- Official guide on [sell.amazon.in](https://sell.amazon.in/). General flow (3rd-party): mobile/email →
  GSTIN (PAN for GST-exempt categories) → bank → identity verification → listing.
- **Non-blocking GST check:** up to 72 h, result by email; seller continues other steps meanwhile
  ([Kanakkupillai](https://www.kanakkupillai.com/learn/sell-on-amazon-india/), 3rd-party).
- **Identity:** in-app "Take a photo of your face" + ID (3–8 min) or a video call with an associate
  ([forum quoting Amazon](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/03a80cda-d340-4823-aeab-792a27300eb5),
  not India-specific); India sellers report video verification
  ([sellercentral.amazon.in forum](https://sellercentral.amazon.in/seller-forums/discussions/t/21bf8102-fe7e-4508-8d35-c9e057dd8b5c));
  video interviews since 2020 ([YourStory, Apr 2020](https://yourstory.com/2020/04/amazon-ecommerce-video-interview-merchants)).
- Bank: method unverified; name must match PAN/GST (3rd-party).
- **Fees:** zero referral fee < ₹300 from 7-Apr-2025
  ([Amazon press](https://press.aboutamazon.com/in/news/small-business/2025/8/amazon-announces-zero-referral-fees-on-over-1-2-crore-products-move-aimed-at-boosting-growth-of-lakhs-of-sellers-across-india)),
  extended to 12.5 crore+ products < ₹1,000 from 16-Mar-2026
  ([Amazon press, Mar 2026](https://press.aboutamazon.com/in/2026/3/amazon-announces-zero-referral-fees-on-over-12-5-crore-products-sellers-to-save-up-to-70-in-fees)).
  **Revenue Calculator** shows fees before the first sale.
- **Catalogue:** barcode scan (UPC/EAN/ISBN) in the Seller App; match an existing ASIN and add only price,
  quantity, shipping; Excel templates
  ([Amazon seller blog](https://sell.amazon.in/seller-blog/four-easy-ways-to-list-your-products-on-amazon),
  [List products](https://sell.amazon.in/sell-online/list-your-products)). 2026 AI tools pre-fill up to 70%
  of a listing from one image or URL ([YourStory, Jun 2026](https://yourstory.com/2026/06/amazon-india-unveils-new-ai-powered-seller-tools), snippet).
- **Languages:** registration & account management in 8 Indian languages + English
  ([Amazon press, Sep 2021](https://press.aboutamazon.com/in/2021/9/amazon-launches-sellers-registration-account-management-services-in-malayalam-telugu-and-bengali)).
- **Assistance / after go-live:** Seller University, Service Provider Network, account management;
  "Try Amazon" ≈ ₹28k benefits incl. ad credits ([sell.amazon.in/try-amazon](https://sell.amazon.in/sell-online/try-amazon));
  New Seller Incentive up to ₹36k (2025) ([Amazon blog](https://sell.amazon.in/seller-blog/all-you-need-to-know-about-new-seller-incentive-2025)).
- **Local Shops on Amazon:** only stated eligibility is owning a physical/kirana shop; shop delivers with
  its own staff ([sell.amazon.in/local-shops](https://sell.amazon.in/sell-online/local-shops-on-amazon));
  2 lakh+ shops in 350 cities ([aboutamazon.in](https://www.aboutamazon.in/md/news/small-business/local-shops-rely-on-amazon-to-expand-beyond-their-neighbourhoods)).
  2025–26 programme details and Smart Stores onboarding: unverified.

**Flipkart Seller Hub**
- "Create a seller account in under 10 minutes with just 1 product and a valid GSTIN"
  ([seller.flipkart.com](https://seller.flipkart.com), snippet). GSTIN validated live; state mismatch with
  pickup address flagged ([aiaccountant](https://www.aiaccountant.com/blog/flipkart-gst-registration-sellers-guide), 3rd-party).
- 2019 revamp: documents cut to **GSTIN + cancelled cheque + signature**; single-step GST verification;
  dashboard shows **profile-completion progress**; **13 regional field teams** across 4,300+ PIN codes
  ([The News Minute, 22-Jul-2019](https://www.thenewsminute.com/atom/flipkart-revamps-seller-onboarding-help-msmes-access-benefits-e-commerce-105903)).
- Fees: zero commission < ₹1,000 from 14-Nov-2025
  ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/flipkart-zero-commission-value-commerce-125111401468_1.html));
  "Settlement Based Pricing" ([BW Retail](https://bwretailworld.com/sector/e-commerce-marketplaces/flipkart-strengthens-seller-growth-with-new-success-programme)).
- Handholding: **New Seller Success Programme** (Jan 2025) — 60 days free help, account manager, 2.3×
  more new sellers succeed ([YourStory, Jun 2025](https://yourstory.com/2025/06/flipkart-new-programme-support-first-time-sellers));
  "Advertise Now, Pay Later" ([YourStory, Jul 2025](https://yourstory.com/2025/07/flipkart-rolls-out-advertise-now-pay-later-to-ease-ad-access-sellers)).
  Bank/KYC method and Seller Hub languages: unverified.

**Meesho Supplier**
- Non-GST sellers since 1-Oct-2023 with a GST **Enrolment ID/UIN** (same state, ₹40 lakh cap); ~25,000
  joined in 2 months ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/meesho-to-onboard-non-gst-registered-small-businesses-to-broaden-seller-base/amp),
  [Business Standard, Dec 2023](https://business-standard.com/amp/companies/news/softbank-backed-meesho-adds-nearly-25-000-non-gst-sellers-in-2-months-123121501111_1.html)).
- Steps (3rd-party): mobile OTP → GSTIN or Enrolment ID → pickup address → bank → catalogue
  ([Qikink PDF, Oct 2026](https://qikink.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/how-to-sell-on-meesho-without-gst-starter-guide.pdf)).
  0% commission (3rd-party). **Supplier Learning Hub** with a video on every page, Hindi version
  ([meesho.io](https://meesho.io/blog/supplier-learning-hub-meesho-app-user-first)). KYC/bank: unverified.

**JioMart seller / JioMart Partner**
- Seller: GST, PAN, bank, business proof, ID; no sign-up fee; 1–15% commission; 10–15 working days
  (3rd-party, [Shiprocket](https://www.shiprocket.in/blog/how-to-become-a-jiomart-seller/)).
- JioMart Partner: B2B wholesale app for kiranas with account managers; kiranas meeting thresholds are
  linked to the consumer app; JioPOS ₹3,000 refundable deposit
  ([The NewsMill, Jul 2021](https://thenewsmill.com/2021/07/jiomart-partner-guwahati-benefit-grocery-stores/)).

### Local / hyperlocal / ONDC

**magicpin** — self-onboarding to "join ONDC in under five minutes" for food merchants; zero commission and
zero onboarding fee in a ₹100 crore campaign (Aug–Oct 2024)
([YourStory, Jul 2024](https://yourstory.com/2024/07/magicpin-invest-rs-100-cr-onboard-1-lakh-new-food-delivery-partners-ondc));
~70k restaurants by Sep 2024. Kirana flow, documents, KYC: unverified.

**ONDC seller apps (Mystore, Paytm/Pai)**
- Mystore: "Sell on ONDC" → verify mobile → business, address, **bank**, store → catalogue → orders;
  "usually 2–3 business days to go live" ([Mystore](https://www.mystore.in/en/topic/seller-registration), snippet;
  [training manual](https://www.mystore.in/s/62ea2c599d1398fa16dbae0a/ms.files/Mystore_Seller_Training_Manual.pdf)).
- **GSTIN auto-fetch:** business name, PAN, type and address pulled from the GST database; without GSTIN,
  enrolment number, same-state only ([help.mystore.in](https://help.mystore.in/topic/seller-registration-and-kyc), snippet).
- Free registration; commission 4–8% (manual vs blog differ). **Master Catalogue**
  ([Mystore blog](https://www.mystore.in/en/blog/5-powerful-features-of-mystore-seller-app-for-enterprises-to-unlock-ondc-networks-potential)).
- Paytm bought seller-side Bitsila (Feb 2024), now "Pai Platforms"
  ([Telangana Today, Apr 2026](https://telanganatoday.com/paytm-e-commerce-renamed-as-pai-platforms-acquires-ondc-seller-firm-bitsila));
  Paytm for Business seller flow: unverified. ONDC interoperable store QR
  ([Indian Retailer](https://indianretailer.com/article/retail-business/ecommerce/how-ondcs-interoperable-qr-code-will-transform-indias-retail)).

**Dukaan** — self-serve store; help-centre **"General checklist for starting a new store"**
([help](https://dukaan.help.center/article/5055-general-checklist-for-starting-a-new-dukaan-store)); add products
→ **share store link on WhatsApp** ([help](https://dukaan.help.center/article/5056-how-do-i-start-a-store-on-dukaan));
storefront in 100+ languages. KYC/fees: unverified.

**DotPe** — QR ordering, web store, POS for small merchants; sales-led onboarding via LeadSquared CRM
([DQ Channels](https://www.dqchannels.com/dotpe-partners-leadsquared-merchant-onboarding)). Steps/KYC: unverified.

**Zomato restaurant partner** — documents (3rd-party): FSSAI, PAN, GST, bank, owner Aadhaar, menu, photos;
online form → documents → executive visit/photoshoot → agreement → live
([Online Legal India](https://www.onlinelegalindia.com/blogs/zomato-registration)); field + tele onboarding
([Channelplay](https://www.channelplay.in/success-story/zomato-restaurant-partner-onboarding)). Onboarding
fee disputed (unverified). Restaurant Services Hub (licences, hiring) —
[Business Today, Jun 2024](https://businesstoday.in/amp/technology/news/story/zomato-rolls-out-restaurant-services-hub-feature-to-all-indian-cities-how-it-works-435114-2024-06-28).

**Swiggy restaurant partner** — official guide: "Partner with us" → restaurant/owner/city → FSSAI, GST, KYC
→ cancelled cheque, menu, bill → **site visit** + agreement
([Swiggy blog, 23-Mar-2024](https://blog.swiggy.com/food/complete-guide-to-partner-with-swiggy/)).
Launchpad (2023): 0% commission month 1 + growth manager
([Business Standard, Mar 2023](https://business-standard.com/article/companies/swiggy-starts-launchpad-for-restaurants-at-0-first-month-commission-123031700829_1.html));
still running in 2025–26: unverified.

## 3. Best-in-class practices

| # | Practice | Who (source) |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Minimum upfront — account in < 10 min with GSTIN + 1 product | Flipkart ([seller.flipkart.com](https://seller.flipkart.com), snippet); Udaan "bare minimum" |
| P2 | Don't block on a slow check — continue while GST verifies | Amazon (3rd-party) |
| P3 | GSTIN auto-fetch prefills name, PAN, type, address | Mystore (snippet) |
| P4 | Live GSTIN check incl. state vs pickup address | Flipkart |
| P5 | Accept non-GST shops (Enrolment ID / PAN), same-state | Meesho, Mystore |
| P6 | Camera-first identity: selfie + ID in-app, video fallback | Amazon |
| P7 | DigiLocker / Aadhaar e-KYC — not confirmed at any benchmarked marketplace; offered by KYC providers ([Cashfree docs](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/secure-id/digilocker/digilocker.md)) | opportunity |
| P8 | Profile-completion progress on the dashboard | Flipkart (2019) |
| P9 | Join a master catalogue entry; add only price & stock | Amazon (ASIN match), Mystore |
| P10 | Barcode scan to list | Amazon Seller App |
| P11 | Photo-to-listing AI (≈70% prefilled) | Amazon India 2026 |
| P12 | Bulk Excel templates | Amazon, Meesho |
| P13 | Fees shown before the first sale (calculator / settlement pricing) | Amazon, Flipkart, Mystore |
| P14 | Zero / low commission at entry | Flipkart, Amazon, Swiggy Launchpad, magicpin |
| P15 | Onboarding in Indian languages | Amazon (8), Meesho (Hindi) |
| P16 | Field agents who onboard at the shop | Flipkart, Swiggy, Zomato |
| P17 | Self-serve with a time promise ("< 5 min") | magicpin, Mystore |
| P18 | Guided first 60 days with an account manager | Flipkart NSSP, Swiggy Launchpad |
| P19 | Launch credits after go-live | Amazon Try Amazon, Flipkart ads credit |
| P20 | Go-live checklist + share link / store QR | Dukaan, ONDC QR |
| P21 | Video help on every onboarding page | Meesho Learning Hub, Amazon SU |
| P22 | Shop-first path for kiranas (own delivery staff) | Amazon Local Shops, JioMart Partner |

**Not confirmed at any competitor — room for GoKesari to lead:** documents explicitly deferred until
before the first payout; DigiLocker / Aadhaar e-KYC for sellers; penny-drop bank verification stated
publicly; WhatsApp onboarding and status updates; real-time verification status beyond e-mail;
agent-assisted sign-up with owner OTP consent.
