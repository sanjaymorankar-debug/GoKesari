# Tile Board everywhere — UI pass (9 Oct 2026)

Follow-up to `README.md` in this folder. Applies the approved design
("Theme 1 Tile Board") to every screen of the four roles and fixes the gaps
from the 8 Oct UI audit, the benchmark and the usability checks.
**UI layer only:** no change to business logic, database structure, APIs,
payment flows or user data. Test results: `TEST_RESULTS_UI_PASS.md`.

## What a user sees

| Role | Home | Changes |
|---|---|---|
| Customer / visitor | `/` | "Do now" on phones: the order on its way and tomorrow's delivery, one tap each. Visitors get two sign-up buttons instead (Sell on GoKesari, Deliver with us). Tiles are single large targets on phones. The board is the whole page: shop list → Shops, price comparison → Shops › Compare prices (`/search/compare`), tomorrow's card → Subscriptions › Tomorrow. |
| Shop owner | `/shop` | Account alerts at the top: suspended, rejected, awaiting approval (with the next step), wallet too low, legal documents due, payout bank. Then "Do now" and 16 tiles (2 columns on phones). The old long dashboard is split into `/shop/manage/{today, products, location, hours, gst-pan, shop-types, registration}`. |
| Admin | `/admin` | "Do now": Approvals, Reviews, Refunds, Escalated. Then 9 tiles, each opening its menu page `/admin/menu/{key}`. The 17-section console is split into `/admin/console/{section}`. |
| Operator | `/admin` | "Do now": Late orders, Shop applications, Open tickets, Rider applications. Then 7 tiles; the 7th, **Catalogue**, collects the catalogue tools operators already had. |

Every other page:
- A one-row header, the same look as the boards, with the EN / हिं / मरा switch and the language kept in the `gk_lang` cookie.
- Staff see a **Board** button. The flat role dropdowns (12–39 links) and the phone drawer are gone.
- Admins and operators no longer see cart, deliver-to or wallet.

## How "one path" works

- **Tile → where its submenus are first.** A tile opens one of two places:
  - its own page, when its submenus are tabs or sections of that page (Orders, Returns, Disputes, Wallet, Payout bank, Offers, Analytics, Inventory, Price updates, and every customer tile);
  - its menu page (`/shop/menu/{key}`, `/admin/menu/{key}`), which lists the board's submenus plus the rest of that area ("More").
- **At most 2 clicks.** Every function is 2 clicks or fewer from the home board, and each board group holds at most 7 entries.
- **Tested.** `tests/unit/tile-board.test.ts` checks that every link of the removed role menus, and every old console and dashboard section, is within two clicks.
- **Same counts as the badges.** The list tabs count exactly what the badges count (`src/lib/board/status-groups.ts`):
  - shop orders: New / Packing / Ready / Out / Done / All;
  - customer orders: Active / Past;
  - returns: Requests / Pickups;
  - disputes: Open / Resolved.
- **Old links still work.** Old `/admin#users`, `/shop#products` and similar links forward to the new pages (`src/components/board/hash-redirect.tsx`).

## Layout rules

- **Phones (below 1024 px).** Shop, admin and operator boards are fixed to `100svh`, and the tile grid shares the height evenly. Each tile is one link (at least 60 px). Header controls are 48 px (44 px below 360 px width). "Do now" and the categories are 48–56 px.
- **Wide screens.** Submenus are separate 36 px chips (mouse and keyboard), with "More (n)" where a menu page has more.
- **Text.** Tile labels are 14–15 px. On screens under 760 / 660 px tall they step down to 13 / 12 px (`globals.css`). Badges are 12 px.
- **Tokens.** One set of board tokens in `globals.css`: `--gk-bg`, `--gk-line`, `--gk-chip`.

## States and feedback

- `src/app/error.tsx` and `src/app/not-found.tsx` show in the user's language, with "Try again" or "Back to board".
- The offline banner is `components/board/offline-banner.tsx`. The navigation progress bar, shown from the tap, is `components/board/navigation-feedback.tsx`.
- There is deliberately **no route-level `loading.tsx`**: it would stream every page, turning HTTP 307 redirects into 200 responses that redirect on the client.
- Destructive one-tap actions now ask first (`components/confirm-button.tsx`): delete address, cancel order, cancel and refund.
