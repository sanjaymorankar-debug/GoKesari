# GoKesari Authorized Dealer — Onboarding Guide

For dealers and their field employees. Works the same on the website and in the GoKesari app.
Last updated 28 Oct 2026.

---

## 1. Become a dealer

1. Sign in at **gokesari.com** (or the app) and open **Become a dealer** (`/dealer/apply`).
2. Choose your type: individual, proprietorship, partnership or company.
3. Fill in your details and verify your **mobile number** with the one-time code.
4. Upload the documents asked for your type — usually PAN, GSTIN (if registered), Shop &
   Establishment, address proof, bank proof (cancelled cheque or statement) and the signed agreement.
5. Add your bank account. GoKesari checks it with a ₹1 deposit.
6. Accept the agreement and submit. GoKesari reviews your application and tells you the result.

Once approved you get:
- **Your dealer code**, for example `GKS-MH-PUN-7K4Q9X`
- **A share link** — `gokesari.com/shop/register?ref=YOUR-CODE` — and a **QR code**

---

## 2. Your employees

On **My dealer page** (`/dealer`) → **Employees**, add each field employee with their name and your
own **Employee ID** for them (for example `E01`).

When a shop registers with your code, it can also give the employee's ID (an optional field).
Each employee's shops and results then appear separately on your board, so you can share
commission with them as you agree.

---

## 3. Bringing shops on board

Three ways — all of them credit the shop to you:

| How | What the shop owner does |
|---|---|
| **Share your link or QR** | Opens it; your code is already filled in; registers and pays the fee |
| **Give your code** | Types your code on the registration form (and your employee's ID, optional) |
| **Register it with the owner** (dealer-assisted) | You fill in the shop; the owner confirms with a one-time code sent to them, then pays |

**Leads:** met a shop that is not ready yet? Record it as a lead with the owner's consent. It is
reserved for you for **30 days**: if the shop registers without a code in that time, it is still
yours. You can hold up to 200 open leads.

**Please note:**
- A shop that is already on GoKesari, or a likely duplicate (same phone, GSTIN, or a similar
  shop next door), earns no commission.
- If you believe a shop is yours but it was credited elsewhere, raise an **attribution claim** from
  your board. GoKesari decides and tells you why.

---

## 4. When does a shop go live?

The shop needs its documents verified (PAN, GSTIN or the "not GST-registered" declaration, Shop
Act; FSSAI for food shops) and its registration fee paid.

Where instant approval is switched on: when the GSTIN and PAN check out with the government
and the fee is paid online, the shop is **approved immediately** and the owner gets an e-mail to
start uploading products. Otherwise GoKesari approves it after review.

Your board shows every shop's stage: documents pending, fee pending, waiting for approval, live.

---

## 5. Your commission

Pilot rates for a standard shop (wholesale rates are set separately):

| When | You earn |
|---|---|
| The shop registers and pays its fee | ₹100 |
| Its documents are verified and it has 10 products listed | ₹150 |
| The shop is approved (goes live) | ₹150 |
| **Total per shop** | **₹400** |

- Your current rates are always shown on your page.
- Commission goes **earned → approved by GoKesari → paid**. Payouts are made by bank transfer to
  your verified account; each shows its bank reference. Download your statement as CSV any time.
- If a shop's fee is refunded or a registration turns out to be a duplicate or fraudulent, its
  commission is reversed (if already paid, it is deducted from your next payout).
- If GoKesari approves a shop before its documents are verified, the ₹150 activation commission
  shows **on hold** until the documents are verified, then it is released automatically.
- There is a per-shop limit on commission.

---

## 6. Your rating and bonus

You start at **5.0**. Your rating rises with the shops you bring that go live:

| Live shops | Rating |
|---|---|
| 0 | 5.0 |
| 200 | 5.5 |
| 250 | 6.0 |
| 500 | 6.5 |

Where the bonus is switched on, every 0.1 above 5.0 adds 0.1% of the shop's fee to your commission.
Your page shows your rating, bonus and how many shops you need for the next step.

---

## 7. Your board

**My dealer page** (`/dealer`) shows:
- your profile, agreement, code, link and QR
- your shops by stage, and registrations still pending
- shops onboarded by day, week and month
- each employee's results, and each area's
- your commission (earned, pending, approved, paid, reversed, on hold) and rating

You only ever see your own shops and results.

---

## 8. Help for your shops

Shops can ask GoKesari to **upload their products** (₹499 per 50 products + GST) or **update their
prices** (₹199 per 50 products + GST, first request each month free); general support is free.
You can raise these for the shops you brought from your board. The shop sees the price first; the
owner pays from the shop wallet or online.

---

## 9. Contact

Questions about your application, commission or payouts: raise a support ticket from your page,
or write to GoKesari support.
