# Competitive analysis: GoKesari vs India's top 10 e-commerce & quick-commerce players (October 2026)

A business-analysis comparison of GoKesari.com with Amazon India (incl. Amazon Now),
Flipkart, Flipkart Minutes, Meesho, BigBasket, Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart,
JioMart and Country Delight.

## Method and caveats

- GoKesari features were read from this repository (Oct 2026). Some are behind
  business rules that are on in the test site and off in production.
- Competitor data comes from public sources as of 16 Oct 2026: company results,
  DRHPs, press releases and trade press. Periods differ by company.
- App ratings come from third-party aggregators and are approximate. "n/v" in the
  tables means not verified from public sources, not that the feature is missing.
- Scores are analyst judgement, 1–5 per criterion. Change them in the workbook's
  Scorecard sheet to test other weightings.
