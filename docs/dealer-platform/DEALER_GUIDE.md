# Becoming and working as a GoKesari dealer

## Apply (gokesari.com/dealer/apply)

1. **Choose your scheme.** Compare the schemes:

   | Scheme | Joining charge | Commission |
   |---|---|---|
   | Free Dealership | free | 20% of each shop's registration fee (one-time) |

   Business Partner and Growth Partner are not open yet.

   Want a franchise zone of your own? Use **Book a franchise zone**.
2. **Fill in your details, verify your mobile, and verify your documents online or upload them.** Answer your scheme's own questions, if it has any.
3. **Pay the joining charge** for a paid scheme. You pay online, and it is refunded to the way you paid if your application is not approved.
4. **Accept the agreement and your scheme's terms, and submit.** Your acceptance is recorded with the date and time.
5. **When approved,** you get your dealer code. Your scheme's terms are fixed for you: later changes by GoKesari do not change them.

## Your dealer page (gokesari.com/dealer)

- **Your scheme:**
  - your terms and their validity;
  - what the scheme has paid you, this month and in total (one-time, team, monthly).
- **Change your scheme:**
  1. Read the other scheme's terms.
  2. Accept them, and pay its charge if there is one.
  3. You move to it as soon as the payment is received; a free scheme applies at once.
  4. Commission already earned does not change.
- **Dealer logo:** upload your logo (when GoKesari has switched this on in your area).
- **Earnings and statements:** as before, with a monthly statement you can download.
