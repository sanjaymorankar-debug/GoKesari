# Seller / customer split — table ownership and cross-database relationships

Generated by `scripts/db-split/relationships.ts` from the database's real foreign keys. Do not edit by hand.

- Seller database (gokesari_seller): **90 tables** (27 of them the product-master schema `pmd`)
- Customer database (gokesari_customer, also the platform core): **100 tables**
- Foreign keys: 444, of which **111 cross the two databases**. PostgreSQL can't enforce those, so each becomes a service-layer check (`src/server/db/split/integrity.ts`) and is verified by `scripts/db-split/run.ts verify` (missing parents per link).

Shared keys are the stable UUIDs (`shop_id`, `shop_product_id`, `product_id`, `order_id`, `user_id`); none of them changes in the split.
